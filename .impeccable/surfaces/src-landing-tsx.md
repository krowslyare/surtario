# Surtario product entrance

Mode: Persuade. Audience: restaurant purchasing operators, with English-first US sample content. One desktop viewport; natural mobile reflow. Primary action opens the existing workspace. No pricing, testimonials, savings claims, sign-up flow or extra sections.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A purchasing decision made tangible. One sample answer changes a visible order, rather than a feature catalogue.

OWN-WORLD: Retain the approved porcelain, aubergine, radish and lime palette, Bricolage display type, Manrope body and existing ingredient photograph. No new global tokens.

STORY: Understand the product, reveal an explicitly fictional supplier answer, see the complete cost, then open Surtario.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Quiet brand/navigation row; generous left-aligned headline and short explanation at left; a large ingredient photograph at right, joined by one readable purchasing slip with the interactive decision. Primary action directly beneath the promise; compact factual footer.

FORM: Code-led extension of the established identity, shaped directly for the requested single-screen entrance. No seed: the narrow format and existing visual world are fixed. The decision interaction is the signature moment; no automatic looping or animated money.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
